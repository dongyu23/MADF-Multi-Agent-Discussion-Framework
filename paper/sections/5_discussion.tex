\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{A minimum reporting norm.}
Our results support a concrete minimum for papers that derive
component-importance claims from LLM-judged multi-agent artifacts:
(1)~score with at least two judges from different model families, and
preferably different vendors; (2)~report pairwise judge agreement and
per-judge offsets alongside the scores; (3)~when judges disagree on the
ordering of the variants, downgrade the component claim to
``undetermined'' or escalate to human judgment, rather than reporting
the single judge's $p$-value. Condition (3) is the escalation idea of
\citet{jung2024trust} applied at the level of conclusions, and (1)--(2)
are the panel idea of \citet{verga2024juries} reduced to its
measurement core.

\paragraph{What the leniency result changes.}
The community's default mitigation for judge bias is family
substitution. Our decomposition shows why that is insufficient here:
the operative effect is a between-judge level shift (offset CI excluding
zero) that survives family substitution, while self-preference---the
effect family substitution targets---is not detected. A judge panel
with a level-calibration step (e.g., anchoring all judges to a common
reference set) is the natural repair; we leave its validation to future
work.

\paragraph{Relationship to general judge-reliability findings.}
That LLM judges are imperfect is established~\cite{reliability2026,
allverdicts2026}. The contribution here is downstream: measuring what
that imperfection does to a specific scientific inference
(component ranking), in a setting (open-ended multi-party dialogue)
where no ground truth anchors the scores, and separating two mechanisms
that prior work treats jointly. The same-machine replication is the
piece that makes the causal claim tight: identical transcripts, one
changed factor.

\paragraph{Threats to validity.}
Single framework and (for the ablation) single topic; five repetitions
per condition; judges share a vendor for two of three judges (a
second-vendor judge is being added; the repository tracks the live
data); persona skills are authored rather than the reference
implementation's; and the protocol reimplementation, while verbatim in
its prompts and rules, replaces the reference agent graph. The
independent-jury record (Appendix~\ref{app:jury}) rates the evidence
``partial'' overall and we have narrowed every claim to what survived
that review.
